1. **Executive Diagnosis & Revenue Leakage Audit:** Plain-business breakdown of why paid ads and store footfall leak before checkout.
2. **North Dhaka 3-Store Moat & Competitor Playbook:** Mapping Glossy Glow's Uttara Square, Police Plaza Concord, and Center Point Airport advantage against same-floor and mall rivals (including Beautyology, Lira Import, La Reina, ShineSkin BD, and Bio-Xin).
3. **Live Website & SEO Proof:** Interactive 12.7s vs 2.3s mobile wait simulator, live site bug callouts, split-domain findings (`glossyglow.com.bd` vs `glossyglowbd.com`), and Google SERP before/after previews.
4. **Social Media & Content Proof:** Live Instagram and YouTube audit showing why Bangla utility content outperforms static catalog posts by 7.5x to 20x.
5. **Growth Loop, 6 Engines & 5 New Revenue Channels:** Interactive Impact/Effort channel explorer and practical revenue expansion beyond paid ads.
6. **90-Day Execution Roadmap & Interactive BDT Simulator:** Clickable 12-week Gantt schedule, 30/60/90-day finish lines, lean 80/20 budget split, and an interactive monthly BDT revenue recovery calculator.
